Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026 Applies to: loancalc.app

Who we are

LoanCalc operates the website loancalc.app, a free suite of financial calculators covering loans, mortgages, savings, refinancing, currency conversion, and live commodity prices. No account or signup is required to use the site.

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), LoanCalc is the data controller for personal data processed in connection with this website. For California privacy law purposes (CCPA/CPRA), LoanCalc is the business responsible for determining how your personal information is used.

Data we collect

We collect the minimum data needed to operate the site and improve it over time. We do not collect your name, email address, or any financial figures you enter into the calculators. Calculator inputs exist only in your browser and are never sent to our servers.

Data collected automatically

  • Server logs. Our web host records standard server log data including your IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps. This is retained for up to 30 days for security and abuse prevention purposes.
  • Analytics data. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics 4 (GA4) records aggregated usage data: pages visited, session duration, country-level location (not precise), device type, and browser. GA4 anonymizes IP addresses by default. This data helps us understand which tools are used most and where to improve.
  • Advertising data. If you accept advertising cookies, Google AdSense and Google Ad Manager record data used to show relevant ads, including your browsing behavior on this and other sites that use Google advertising services.

Data you provide

  • Preferences. If you change your currency or language in the Settings panel, that preference is saved to your browser's local storage (loancalc_prefs). This stays in your browser; we never receive it.
  • Consent record. If you are in the EU/EEA and see a consent dialog, your choices are stored in your browser by Google Funding Choices (Google's certified consent management platform) to avoid re-prompting on every visit.

How we use your data

We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis to do so. The table below explains each processing activity.

Third-party services

We share limited data with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing their use of that data.

Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

Provided by Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. GA4 processes anonymized usage data for analytics purposes. Data is transferred to Google servers in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Google Privacy Policy.

GA4 loads on all pages with analytics_storage: 'denied' by default. EU/EEA users can withdraw analytics consent via the Google consent dialog; on withdrawal, future GA4 events will be withheld.

Google AdSense and Ad Manager

Provided by Google LLC. AdSense and Ad Manager display advertising on this site. If you consent to advertising cookies, Google may use behavioral data from this site and other sites to show personalized ads. If you decline advertising cookies, ads may still appear but will not be personalized. Google Privacy Policy | Manage ad settings.

AdSense loads on all pages to enable auto ads. Personalized ad serving (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) requires your consent and remains denied by default until granted via the Google consent dialog shown to EU/EEA users.

Google Funding Choices (Consent Management)

For users in the EU/EEA, we use Google Funding Choices — Google's free, IAB TCF-certified consent management platform — to collect and record your consent choices. It is delivered automatically via the AdSense script and does not require a separate third-party tag. Google Privacy Policy.

Frankfurter API (Exchange Rates)

Currency exchange rates are fetched from the Frankfurter API (api.frankfurter.app), a public financial data service. No personal data is sent in these requests — only the requested currency codes.

Web hosting infrastructure

The site is hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Cloudflare processes IP addresses and request metadata as part of delivering the site. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Manage your cookie preferences

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies directly. Blocking necessary cookies may affect site functionality (such as storing your preferences).
  • Google: Manage Google's use of your data for advertising at adssettings.google.com. Opt out of GA4 tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Your rights under GDPR (EU and EEA)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you the following rights with respect to your personal data. These rights apply to data where LoanCalc acts as a data controller.

Right of access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it.

Right to erasure

Request that we delete your personal data where there is no compelling reason to continue processing it.

Right to portability

Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller.

Right to rectification

Request that we correct any inaccurate personal data we hold about you without undue delay.

Right to restriction

Request that we restrict processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

Right to object

Object to processing based on legitimate interest, including for direct marketing purposes.

Right to withdraw consent

Withdraw consent for analytics or advertising at any time via the cookie settings. Withdrawal does not affect prior processing.

Right to complain

Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (DPA). Find your DPA at edpb.europa.eu.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in the Contact section. We will respond within 30 days.

Note on practical scope: Because LoanCalc collects minimal personal data (primarily server logs and, where consented, GA4 analytics), many of these rights may return limited results. For example, we cannot identify your analytics data because GA4 anonymizes identifiers. We can delete consent records and stop processing immediately upon request.

California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) grant you specific rights regarding your personal information.

Categories of personal information collected

In the last 12 months, LoanCalc has collected the following categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers: IP addresses (via server logs, for up to 30 days).
  • Internet or other electronic network activity: Pages visited, session duration, browser type (via Google Analytics, where consented).
  • Geolocation data: Country-level only, inferred from IP address (not precise location).
  • Commercial information: None. We do not process financial data you enter into the calculators.

Sale and sharing of personal information

LoanCalc does not sell your personal information to data brokers or third parties for monetary consideration.

However, when you accept advertising cookies, we share your browsing data with Google AdSense/Ad Manager for the purpose of cross-context behavioral advertising. Under CCPA/CPRA, this sharing may be considered a "sale or sharing" of personal information.

Your right to opt out

You have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise this right, you can:

  • Decline or withdraw advertising cookies via the cookie settings on this page or via the consent banner on any LoanCalc page.
  • Use a browser or extension that sends the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. LoanCalc honors GPC signals where technically feasible.
  • Manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Additional California rights

  • Right to know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months.
  • Right to delete: Request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights. All LoanCalc tools remain free regardless of your privacy choices.

To exercise California rights, contact us at the address in the Contact section. We will respond within 45 days, as required by CCPA.

International data transfers

LoanCalc is accessible worldwide. When you use analytics or advertising cookies, your data is transferred to and processed by Google LLC on servers in the United States.

Google LLC participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF), which provides a legal mechanism for transferring EU personal data to the United States in compliance with GDPR. You can verify Google's DPF participation at dataprivacyframework.gov.

Server log data (IP addresses) is processed on Cloudflare infrastructure, which may route traffic through servers in multiple countries. Cloudflare is also a DPF participant.

Data retention

  • Server logs: Retained for up to 30 days, then deleted.
  • GA4 analytics data: Retained by Google for 14 months (our configured retention period). Aggregated reports may be retained indefinitely.
  • Advertising data: Retained by Google per their data retention policies (typically 13 months for most advertising identifiers).
  • Consent records (Google Funding Choices): Stored in your browser for up to 1 year, or until you clear your cookies.
  • Local preferences: Stored in your browser's local storage until you clear it or reset your browser data.

Children's privacy

LoanCalc is a general-audience financial tool not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/EEA, where a higher age threshold applies for consent).

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated via a notice on the site. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Continued use of LoanCalc after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy-related requests, questions, or complaints, contact us at:

  • Website: loancalc.app
  • Privacy requests: privacy@loancalc.app

Response times: GDPR requests are handled within 30 days. CCPA requests are handled within 45 days. We may extend these periods in complex cases and will notify you if an extension applies.
